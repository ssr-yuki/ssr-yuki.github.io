## Biography

Yuki Onishi is a Project Assistant Professor at the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan.

Research Interests: Control Theory, Humanoid Robotics, and Science Communication

> Yuki Onishi (born in 1998) is a Project Researcher at the National Institute of Informatics (NII), Japan. He received his BEng, MEng, and DEng degrees in Systems and Control Engineering from Tokyo Institute of Technology, Japan, in 2019, 2021, and 2024, respectively. During his graduate study, he was a principal investigator of the strategic basic research program ACT-X by Japan Science and Technology Agency (JST) from 2020 to 2023, a research fellow at Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) from 2023 to 2024, and a visiting graduate student at the Department of Engineering, University of Cambridge, UK, in 2023. Prior to joining the NII, he was a Principal Research Scientist at Chiba Institute of Technology, Japan. He was also a Visiting Research Fellow at the School of Engineering, Institute of Science Tokyo, Japan, from 2024 to 2026.
>
> He is a member of the Robotics Society of Japan (RSJ), the Japan Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (JSIAM), and the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), where he belongs to the Robotics and Automation Society (IEEE-RAS) and the Control Systems Society (IEEE-CSS).
